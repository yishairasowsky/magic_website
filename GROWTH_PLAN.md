## Priority 1 — Referrals (Highest ROI, Lowest Cost)

These are your fastest path to bookings. You've performed for 300+ people at Camp Adirim, done RCCS twice, performed at sheva berachos with 80 guests. Many of those people haven't heard from you since.

### 1a. WhatsApp follow-up with past clients
- After every show, message the organizer 2–3 days later: *"Thanks again for having me — loved the crowd! If you ever need entertainment for another event, or know someone who does, I'd love to help."*
- Keep a simple list of every organizer's number. This alone can double referrals.

### 1b. Ask directly for reviews on Google
- Your schema says 5.0 stars / 12 reviews — but where are these actually posted? If it's not Google Business Profile, fix that first (see Priority 2).
- After each show, send a short message: *"If you have 2 minutes, a Google review would mean a lot — here's the link: [your Google profile link]"*
- Target: get to 30+ Google reviews. This is the single most visible trust signal for people searching "magician Israel."

### 1c. Referral from institutional clients
- RCCS, Zichron Menachem, Bet Elazraki, Lone Soldiers, Kfar Shaul — these organizations know each other and attend each other's events.
- Email the coordinator at each one: *"We had a great show. Do you have colleagues at other organizations who plan events? Happy to offer a referral discount."*
- One email per org, one time. Low effort, potentially several new bookings.

This Lag BaOmer I had the honor of performing mentalism for IDF soldiers at two army bases — one in Maccabim and one in Hashmonaim.

Two shows, 60 soldiers, and a whole lot of mind reading.

These guys put everything on the line for us. The least I can do is blow their minds for an evening. Watching a room full of soldiers go from "yeah right" to "HOW?!" — that never gets old.

To every soldier serving and protecting — thank you. It was a privilege to be there.

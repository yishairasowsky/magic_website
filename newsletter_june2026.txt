Subject: Chodesh Tov -- and a story from two army bases

Wishing you a healthy and blessed Rosh Chodesh Tammuz.

This Lag BaOmer I performed for IDF soldiers at two bases -- one in Maccabim, one in Hashmonaim. Two shows, 60 soldiers, and a whole lot of mind reading.

These guys deal in real stakes every day. They walked in skeptical. Watching a room full of soldiers go from "yeah right" to "HOW?!" -- that never gets old.

One of them wrote to me afterwards:

"Every segment, you managed to surprise the audience and leave us with our mouths open."

He also offered to pass my details along to contacts in Modi'in and Beit Shemesh -- which honestly meant as much to me as the show itself.

If you have an event coming up -- a company evening, a family celebration, anything -- I'd love to be part of it. And if you know someone who does, feel free to pass this along.

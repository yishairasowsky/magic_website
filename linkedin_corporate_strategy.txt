LINKEDIN STRATEGY — GETTING CORPORATE BOOKINGS DIRECTLY

STEP 1: FIX YOUR LINKEDIN PROFILE

Headline: "Mentalist & Mind-Reader | Corporate Events, Private Dinners & Luxury Entertainment | Jerusalem"

About section (keep it short):
"I perform mind-reading at corporate events, team dinners, and private functions across Israel. No stage, no setup — I walk through the room and tell your guests what they're thinking. Companies like [any names you can drop] have booked me for client appreciation dinners, team events, and product launches. If your next event needs something people will actually talk about afterward — let's chat."

STEP 2: CONNECT WITH THE RIGHT PEOPLE

Search LinkedIn for these job titles + "Jerusalem" or "Israel":
- HR Manager
- Office Manager
- People & Culture
- Employee Experience
- Event Manager
- Executive Assistant (to CEOs/VPs — they plan the dinners)
- Marketing Manager (they run client events)

Target companies with Jerusalem offices: Check Point, Mobileye, Bank Leumi tech division, Deloitte, PwC, EY, KPMG, law firms (Meitar, Herzog, Barnea), Teva, pharma companies.

Send a connection request with a note (keep it under 200 characters):
"Hi [name], I'm a mentalist based in Jerusalem — I perform at corporate events and team dinners. Would love to connect."

STEP 3: POST CONTENT THAT ATTRACTS CORPORATE BUYERS

Post 2-3 times per week. Types of posts that work:

1. Short performance clips (15-30 sec) with caption like: "Last week at a corporate dinner for [company/industry]. The CFO's face when I told him the number he was thinking of." Tag the venue location.

2. Behind-the-scenes: "Setting up for a 200-person tech company event tonight. The brief was 'make the CEO's speech less boring.' Challenge accepted." (Even if slightly embellished — it positions you.)

3. Testimonial screenshots: Post a WhatsApp message from a happy client (with permission) and write "This is why I do what I do."

4. Direct pitch posts (once every 2 weeks max): "Planning a team event or client dinner? I perform mind-reading that makes your guests the star of the show. No stage needed — I walk through the room. DM me or 054-848-2245."

STEP 4: DM STRATEGY

After connecting, wait 3-5 days, then send:

"Hey [name], thanks for connecting. Quick question — does [company] ever do team events or client dinners? I perform mind-reading at corporate events and I'm always looking to connect with people who plan these things. No pressure at all, just wanted to put myself on your radar."

If they respond positively, offer a free 5-minute Zoom demo: "I can show you exactly what I do in 5 minutes on a Zoom call — it works even over video. Want to try it?"

STEP 5: LINKEDIN ADS (optional, later)

Once you have 3-4 strong video clips, run a small LinkedIn ad campaign ($5-10/day) targeting:
- Job titles: HR, Office Manager, Executive Assistant, Event Planner
- Location: Israel
- Company size: 50+ employees
- Creative: 15-second clip + "Book a mentalist for your next team event"

WHAT CHANGES COMPARED TO THE EMAILS ALREADY SENT

The emails sent so far introduce Yishai and ask nothing specific, which makes the easy answer "ok, thanks" or no reply. These versions do four things differently. They open with the result the reader gets, not with who Yishai is. They state what he does in one confident line, no hedging words like "I'd like to introduce" or "if it's ever useful". They remove all risk with one concrete offer (a short live demonstration, or a trial). And they end with one small question that is easy to answer yes to, with the two options already written out, because "what day works" gets ignored and "Tuesday or Wednesday" gets answered. The tone stays calm and warm, no exclamation marks and no pressure, but it assumes the meeting will happen.

Proof goes near the end, one line, and the full references are offered. This keeps to the earlier rule of not leading with testimonials.

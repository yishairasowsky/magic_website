PITCH ANGLE
-----------
All of these institutions organize events where entertainment is relevant: PTA fundraising nights, parent visiting weekends (seminaries), graduation events, Chanukah parties, end-of-year dinners, and community gatherings. The Anglo parent population is your exact demographic -- English-speaking, event-going, connected to luxury hospitality circles.

Approach: Email the office or PTA contact, mention you are a Jerusalem-based mentalist who performs at private events, and ask if they have upcoming parent events or fundraisers where live entertainment would add value.

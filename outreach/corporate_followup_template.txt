Corporate Event Planner Follow-Up Template
==========================================

--- VERSION A (after prior conversation) ---

Good morning. I know you are busy, so no pressure -- just wanted to follow up about entertaining your staff at the office. As we discussed, I specialize in fun and interactive mentalism demonstrations. Let me know what day and time could work.

Thanks,
Yishai


--- VERSION B (cold / no prior meeting) ---

Good morning. I know you are busy, so no pressure -- just wanted to reach out about entertaining your staff at the office. I specialize in fun and interactive mentalism demonstrations, which tend to be a big hit at corporate events. Let me know if this sounds interesting and what timing might work.

Thanks,
Yishai


--- NOTES ---
- Use Version A for follow-ups after a real conversation
- Use Version B for cold outreach or after a trade show / brief encounter
- Keep it short, no urgency, no exclamation marks
- Hebrew version below for Israeli event planners who prefer Hebrew

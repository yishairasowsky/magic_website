SAMPLE EMAIL APPROACH
---------------------
Subject line: Entertainment for your next event

Keep the same structure as the Hatzalah email -- lead with appreciation for their work,
offer your services for fundraising events / staff evenings / families in need,
brachah closing, short contact info. No hard sell.

For hospitals and chesed orgs, the "families in need" angle resonates most.
For corporate/chambers, lead with team-building/holiday party angle.
For shuls and yeshivot, Purim is a major season -- start outreach Jan/Feb.

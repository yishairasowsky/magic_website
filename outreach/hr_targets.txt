HOW TO FIND THE EMAIL
----------------------
1. Find the HR person's name on LinkedIn
2. The email pattern at most Israeli companies is:
   firstname.lastname@company.com  OR  firstinitial+lastname@company.com
   (e.g. mayb@amot.co.il suggests first name + last initial)
3. Alternatively, call the company switchboard and ask for the HR department email

NOTES
------
- Jerusalem/Beit Shemesh area companies are easier for you logistically
- Banks and insurance companies tend to have the biggest event budgets
- Clalit alone has 40,000+ employees -- enormous potential
- Focus on companies with Jerusalem offices since you're local

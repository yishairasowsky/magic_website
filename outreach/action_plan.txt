OUTREACH ACTION PLAN
====================
Goal: Get booked for events to build experience.
Do these in order. Do one or two a day, don't rush.


STEP 1 -- RCCS Israel (warm lead, do this first)
-------------------------------------------------
Action: WhatsApp Miriam Horowitz directly.

What to say:
"Hi Miriam, hope you're doing well. I'm reaching out because I've been
growing my magic and mentalism business and I'm looking to perform at
more events for organizations like RCCS. Would you be able to connect
me with whoever handles events or programming at RCCS Israel? I'd love
to offer my services for a family day or staff evening. Thank you so
much."

Why first: She already knows your work and wrote a testimonial. This
is not a cold call. She will likely connect you directly.


STEP 2 -- Shalva (best cold lead)
----------------------------------
Action: CALL first. Then follow up by email.

Call: 02-651-9555, press or ask for ext. 123
Best time to call: Sunday-Thursday, 9am-12pm

What to say on the phone:
"Shalom, I'm a professional magician and mentalist based near Jerusalem.
I heard that Shalva hosts a monthly entertainment program for your
participants. I'd love to offer a performance -- either as a volunteer
or for a small fee. Who should I speak with about that?"

Then listen. If they say send an email, ask for the specific email
address and the name of the person to address it to.

Follow-up email to info@shalva.org:
Subject: Entertainment for Shalva's monthly program

"Shalom,

I'm a professional close-up magician and mentalist based near Jerusalem.
I understand that Shalva regularly hosts entertainment programs for your
participants and their families, and I would love to be part of that.

I specialize in close-up magic and interactive mentalism -- the kind of
experience that works beautifully in a small group or one-on-one setting.
It's personal, fun, and creates genuine moments of delight.

I'd be happy to perform at an upcoming event, whether as a volunteer or
at a reduced rate. Please let me know if there's a good time to speak.

Thank you for the incredible work you do.

Yishai Rasowsky
054-848-2245
www.yishai.show"

GENERAL RULES FOR ALL CALLS
-----------------------------
- Call Sunday-Thursday only, 9am-12pm is best
- Speak Hebrew or English depending on who picks up
- Never leave more than one voicemail
- If they say "send us an email" -- ask for the specific person's name
  to address it to, then send within the hour
- After any call, make a note of who you spoke to and what they said
- Follow up once by email after 5-7 days if no response. Then move on.


TRACKING
---------
Keep a simple list:
Org | Contacted | Date | Method | Response | Follow-up date

Do not chase any single org more than twice. There are plenty of leads.

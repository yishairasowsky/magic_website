KRAUSS OPENER
=============

Let's start with something simple.

Everyone stand up, right where you are.

Without saying a word -- think of a two-digit number, less than 50, with both digits odd and both digits different. So 11 is no good -- both digits are the same. But 15 would be fine. If your number is 37 or 35 -- you did well, please sit down.

[pause, watch who sits]

For those still standing -- think of a color of the rainbow.

[pause]

I'm getting a strong sense of one color -- actually two. If you are thinking of red or blue, please sit down. You did very well.

[pause]

For those still standing -- think of two simple geometric shapes. Something simple, like a square or rectangle -- but not those. Two shapes, one inside the other.

I see a circle. And inside it, a triangle. If you thought of a circle or a triangle, please be seated.

[one or a few people remain standing]

[to the last person standing] Please tell everyone your name.

[NAME] has survived three rounds. And because of that, your thoughts are the hardest for me to read.

Here is what we're going to do. When I say one-two-three-CLAP, everyone in the room is going to slap their hands together. In that moment, I want you to picture any single object in your mind. Don't say it out loud. Audience -- are we ready?

[spread hands, cue the room]

ONE. TWO. THREE. CLAP.

[pause, turn to the spectator]

Just yes or no -- you're thinking of an animal, aren't you?

[audience reacts -- wave them down like Dunninger]

[write down first-choice animal on pad]

Tell the truth to everybody, and please only answer yes or no -- when you first thought of this, did you think of one animal, and then switch to a different one?

[they say yes -- audience reacts -- wave them down, cross out first guess, write the correct one]

The first creature I thought of lives in [HABITAT OF FIRST ANIMAL].

But the one you settled on is a bit different. It lives [DESCRIBE HABITAT OF SECOND ANIMAL].

[cap the marker]

Tell everyone out loud -- what was the first animal you thought of?

[they answer]

And your final choice was a --?

[they answer -- turn the pad around as you say:]

You did very well tonight.

[to the room]

A huge round of applause for [NAME]. She had no idea this was going to happen to her. Neither did I, to be honest.

[teach her to take a bow]

No no -- you are getting applause. You have to accept it. Here's how it works. You stand front and center, you extend both arms out and down -- and as you slowly raise them, the room gets louder and louder. Try it.

[let it play out]

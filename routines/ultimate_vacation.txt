ULTIMATE VACATION + CLOCK
=========================

I want you to plan a vacation. Right now, in your head. Fantasy vacation -- no budget, no limits.

I need four things from you.

First -- your name. Second -- one famous person, living or dead, that you'd want to meet on this vacation. Third -- where in the world. And fourth -- one question you'd ask them if you could.

[spectator answers all four]

Now one more thing. The time of the meeting.

[clock trick -- spectator stops you at a number]

You stopped me at [NUMBER]. Your meeting is at [TIME] o'clock.

[open the prediction]

I wrote this before the show began. Your name. [FAMOUS PERSON]. [PLACE]. [QUESTION]. [TIME] o'clock.

[pause]

Out of every destination in the world, every person who ever lived, every question you could have asked -- that is exactly what I wrote down before this evening began.

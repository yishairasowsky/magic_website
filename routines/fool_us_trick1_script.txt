FOOL US SUBMISSION - Trick #1: Card Routine
=============================================

STACK (top to bottom):
1. 10 of Spades (unsigned)
2. 10 of Spades (duplicate, pre-signed by Yishai before going on stage)
3. Ace of Hearts
4. Black Queen
5. Black Queen
6. Ace of Diamonds
7. 5 of Hearts
8. Black Two
9. Black Two
10. 5 of Diamonds
11. Rest of deck

FORMAT:
- SAY: = lines to speak
- DO: = physical actions/moves
- NOTE: = internal notes/reminders

PERFORMANCE:
-----------

PHASE 1: THE TRANSPOSITION

SAY: "I have two cards here. This is the 10 of Hearts. Ten -- like giving someone a perfect score. And the heart -- because the best compliment comes from here."
DO: Show the 10 of Hearts openly.

SAY: "And this is the 10 of Spades. Same idea, different color."
DO: Show the 10 of Spades openly.

SAY: "Teller, I'm giving you the heart. I know you have a lot to say... you just keep it all in here."
DO: Hand Teller the 10 of Hearts and a marker. Gesture to chest warmly.

SAY: "Sign it for me."
DO: Teller signs the 10 of Hearts.

SAY: "And I'll sign mine."
DO: Sign the 10 of Spades on the border. Place it face-up on the deck, necktied so they can't see the pre-signed duplicate underneath.

DO: Double turnover -- flip top two cards as one, showing the pre-signed 10 of Spades (duplicate). The real one is now face-down on top.

SAY: "Penn, hold on to this one for me."
DO: Deal the top card (actually Teller's signed 10 of Hearts, face-down? Or the unsigned 10 of Spades?) to Penn.

BOOK TEST -- CUE SHEET
Performer = Performer   Spectator = the relevant person on stage

PREMISE:
Researchers claim that a concentrated thought leaves a detectable trace --
something that can be sensed, if you know how to look for it.

BEFORE THE SHOW:
Ask one audience member to bring any book from home.

PEOPLE NEEDED:
- Book Owner (brings the book)
- Page Picker (stands far stage left)
- Line caller (shouts from audience)
- Word caller (shouts from audience)

PEEK METHOD:
Stand center. Page Picker stands far stage left, both facing the audience.
Flip pages toward him. Number you see on the flashback peek is 1 less than his -- add 1.


1.  Performer: Call up Book Owner. Hold book up briefly for the room to see.

2.  Performer: Call up Page Picker. Guide him to far stage left.
               "Stand over there. I'll flip through -- stop me anywhere."

3.  Performer: Flip slowly toward Page Picker -- GET PEEK on the stop.
               "See the page number? Lock it in your mind. Don't say a word."

4.  Performer: To audience -- point to Line caller.
               "Line number -- shout one out."
               Line caller shouts a number.

5.  Performer: Point to Word caller.
               "Word number."
               Word caller shouts a number.

6.  Performer: Hand book to Book Owner.
               "Page [NUMBER]. Line [NUMBER]. Word [NUMBER].
                Open it up. Find the word. Point to it -- don't say it yet."

7.  Book Owner: Opens book, finds the word, points to it silently.

8.  Performer: Pause. Reach into pocket -- produce prediction card face-down.
               "Was I in the right area, close, or exactly on the word?"

9.  Book Owner: Answers.

10. Performer: "Say it out loud."

11. Book Owner: Reads the word aloud.

12. Performer: Turn card around so the whole room sees it at once.
               Hold the silence.
               "I wrote down what I sensed."

13. Performer: Send everyone back naturally. No applause cue.

TRANSITION:
"That was a word from a book. Now -- something more personal."

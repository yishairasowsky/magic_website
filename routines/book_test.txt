BOOK TEST
=========

[SETUP: Ask one audience member before the show to bring any book from home.
You need 4-5 people on stage: BOOK OWNER, PAGE PICKER (far stage left),
and 1-2 people from the audience for line and word numbers, plus optional GUARDS.
Bring them all up at once before you start talking.]

[PEEK METHOD: Stand center. PAGE PICKER stands far stage left, both facing the audience.
Flip pages toward him. The number you see on the flashback peek is 1 less than his -- add 1.]


[Book owner brings the book up. Hold it up briefly for the room to see.]

[BOOK OWNER] chose this book. I haven't seen it until right now.

[to PAGE PICKER -- guide him to far stage left]

Stand over there. I'll flip through -- stop me anywhere you like.

[flip slowly toward him -- get the peek on the stop]

See the page number? Lock it in your mind.

[hand book to GUARDS if using them, or set it aside]

[to the audience -- point to LINE PICKER]

Line number -- shout one out.

[LINE PICKER calls a number]

[to another audience member -- WORD PICKER]

Word number.

[WORD PICKER calls a number]

[to BOOK OWNER]

Page [NUMBER]. Line [NUMBER]. Word [NUMBER].
Open it up. Find the word. Point to it -- don't say it yet.

[she opens the book and points -- guards or nearby people lean in and see it]

[pause -- look at her, then slowly reach into your pocket and produce the prediction card face-down]

I want to ask you something first. Was I in the right area, close, or exactly on the word?

[she answers]

Say it out loud.

[she reads the word]

[turn the card around so the whole room sees it at once]

[hold the silence]

I wrote that before tonight.

[done -- no bow line, no applause cue, no epilogue -- let the room decide]

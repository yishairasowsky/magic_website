BOOK TEST
=========

[SETUP: Before the show, ask one audience member to bring any book from home. You have never seen it. You need 5 people on stage: BOOK OWNER, PAGE PICKER (stands far stage left), LINE PICKER, WORD PICKER, and 1-2 GUARDS who hold the book at the end. Bring them all up at the start of this routine.]

[PEEK METHOD: Stand center stage. PAGE PICKER stands to your far left. Flip pages toward him. Because of the angle, the page number you see on the flashback peek is 1 less than what he sees -- add 1 to get his number.]


[Invite the book owner to bring the book to center stage. Hold it up for the audience.]

Earlier this evening, I asked [BOOK OWNER] to bring a book from home. Any book she wanted. I have never touched this book, never seen it until this moment.

[to PAGE PICKER]

Sir -- I need you to stand all the way over there on the left.

[guide him to far stage left, both of you facing the audience]

I'm going to flip through the pages slowly. The moment you want me to stop, say stop. Anywhere you like.

[flip slowly toward him -- get the flashback peek as he calls stop]

[he calls stop]

Good. Can you see the page number? Take a look. Remember it -- don't say a word.

[he nods -- hand the book to the GUARDS]

[to the GUARDS]

Hold this together. Don't let it out of your hands.

[to the audience -- picking LINE PICKER]

You -- what line on that page? Shout out any number.

[LINE PICKER calls a number]

[to the audience -- picking WORD PICKER]

And you -- which word on that line? Give me a number.

[WORD PICKER calls a number]

[to BOOK OWNER]

[BOOK OWNER] -- please take the book from the guards, open to page [PAGE NUMBER], go to line [LINE NUMBER], count to word number [WORD NUMBER]. Don't say it out loud yet. Just point to it so the guards can see it too.

[she finds it and points -- guards lean in -- everyone on stage can see the word]

[pause -- look at her, then at the guards, then at the room]

The page was stopped by this gentleman, completely freely. The line was called out by someone in the audience. The word by someone else. Nobody coordinated. And [BOOK OWNER] brought this book from her own shelf -- it has nothing to do with me.

[to BOOK OWNER]

Tell me honestly -- was I far off, somewhere in the neighborhood, or exactly on the word?

[she answers -- if she says exactly, cue her:]

Say it out loud.

[she reads the word]

[reach into your pocket and produce the prediction card -- hold it face-down for a moment]

Before this evening began -- before anyone walked into this room -- I wrote one word on this card.

[turn it around to show the room]

[hold the silence -- don't rush it]

That book has been on her shelf for years. That page was stopped by a stranger. That line and that word were called out by two more people, chosen at random. And yet.

[lead the applause -- send everyone back to their seats warmly]

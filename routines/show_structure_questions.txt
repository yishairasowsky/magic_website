SHOW STRUCTURE — QUESTIONS TO THINK THROUGH
============================================


THE PREMISE
-----------

1. Who are you when you walk on stage? Not your name -- your identity in the world of the show.
   Are you a scientist who studies the mind? A mentalist with a gift he didn't ask for?
   Someone who reads people for a living? Something else entirely?
Honestly I want to make people laugh their heads off as hard as possible
2. What is the show "about"? If someone had to describe it in one sentence the next morning,
   what would you want them to say? Not "he guessed stuff" -- but what's the deeper idea?
   (Examples: the power of human connection, how much we reveal without knowing it,
   the line between coincidence and fate, etc.)
I don't know but I want them to see impossible stuff and hilarious stuff and I want to book me again like you gotta have this guy at your event That's what they should be telling their neighbors and friends and everyone
3. Do you want the audience to believe you have a real power -- or is it OK if they
   think it's a skill, or somewhere in between? This changes everything about how you talk.
No I don't want people to think I around those supernatural power but I want them to think I have an amazing power of entertaining

THE AUDIENCE'S JOURNEY
-----------------------

4. How do you want the audience to feel at the very start, before you've done anything?
   Curious? Warm? A little nervous? What's the mood you're setting?
I don't know how they're supposed to feel at the beginning but I want to amaze them and most of all entertain them I want to make them laugh their heads off
5. How do you want them to feel in the middle of the show -- when they're deep in it?
I don't know but I want them to feel like anticipation excitement exhilaration laughs everything totally totally entertaining Maybe Ken Weber Maximum Entertainment is good advice
6. How do you want them to feel at the very end, walking out?
   (Amazed? Moved? Unsettled? Joyful? Something they can't name?)

THE TRANSITIONS
---------------

12. Right now each routine just ends and the next one begins. What's missing in that gap?
    Is it a line? A pause? A shift in energy? A callback to something earlier?
Well yeah I definitely need a line at least so there's no dead time
13. Should the transitions feel like chapters in a book (clear breaks),
    or like one continuous stream where you barely notice the seam?
I don't know if there should be breaks I would like there to be no dead time at all Hey listen you know what Conniver did for his psychic routine he would just say now I'd like everyone to think of some secret information Now there's a woman there's someone over here thinking about their birthday if I can guess that and then I don't know if I can tell them the year and they would just go right into it it seems like there's no transition at all
14. Is there a single image, word, or idea that could be the thread running through
    the whole show -- something you introduce early and return to at the end?
Well whoever's the guest of honor of the show I would like to make them the star For example a bar mitzvah or an anniversary or the birthday person

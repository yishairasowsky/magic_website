WHITE DWARF  [opener]
=====================

[METHOD:
  - Props: small envelope, large envelope, windowed duplicate of small envelope (in pocket),
    card, pen, marker, placard/board
  - The windowed duplicate looks identical to the small envelope from the front.
    The peek window is on the back.

  Full sequence:
  1. Spectator examines both envelopes, then hands them back as you give them the card and pen.
     You naturally retain both envelopes -- no announcement, no explanation.
  2. Leave the room with both envelopes. Switch the small for the windowed duplicate outside.
     Leave the large envelope outside too -- you'll bring back only the windowed small one.
  3. From outside, announce the instructions (see script below).
     Spectator writes while you're gone. They signal when done.
  4. Return. You're holding the windowed small envelope.
     Hold it open yourself, flap toward them -- "slide your card in."
     You control the envelope at all times so it stays flat and window stays hidden.
     Seal it yourself.
  5. YOU place the small envelope into the large one -- this is when you get the peek.
     The window faces you as you slide it in. You see the word.
  6. Reveal via placard -- direct confident statement for the opener,
     no Magician in Trouble structure (save that for later in the show).]

[TARGET TIME: 3 minutes. No more.]


There's a phenomenon called remote viewing -- the idea that a trained mind can perceive
something at a distance, with no physical connection to it. We're going to test that now.

[hold up both envelopes briefly]

Take a look at these.

[hand both envelopes to spectator -- 5 seconds -- as they hand them back, give them the card and pen, naturally retaining the envelopes]

I'm going to step outside. Write down anything.

[point to the card -- no further emphasis on what they write]

Gaze at it for a few seconds when you're done.

[exit -- switch small for windowed duplicate outside]

[from outside the room, loud enough to be heard:]

It could be anything -- a name, a place, a word, a number, a drawing.
Whatever comes to mind. When you're done, turn the card face-down and raise your hand.

[audience signals you -- re-enter with windowed small envelope]

[hold the envelope open yourself, flap toward them]

Slide it in.

[they insert the card -- you seal the envelope]

[as you place the small envelope into the large one -- get the peek -- you now know the word]

[pocket or set aside the large envelope -- it is never opened]

Just to confirm -- you wrote that completely on your own, right? I had no part in choosing it?

[they confirm -- pick up the marker and placard]

[write the word on the placard -- face it toward yourself]

[hand the placard face-down to the spectator]

Hold that. Don't look at it yet.

[step back -- face them]

[state the word confidently]

You wrote [WORD].

[beat -- let it land]

Turn it around.

[they show the placard to the room]

[silence]

[done]

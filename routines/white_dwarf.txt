WHITE DWARF: 3 minutes

PREMISE:
A strong mind can project a thought so vividly it becomes almost visible -- like an image
on a screen. After the spectator locks in their thought privately, we stand together on stage
and Yishai absorbs the projected image and names it.


PROPS:
- Billet + pen
- Envelopes: 1 large, 2 small (one windowed duplicate)
- Whiteboard + dry erase marker


PEOPLE:
- 1 spectator on stage for the full routine
- Natural exit after reveal, no applause cue


TRANSITION TO NEXT:
TBD -- depends on running order. Placeholder:
"That image came from inside your mind. Now -- what if it's not an image, but a name..."


PATTER:

1. INTRO
   "A strong mind can project a thought so vividly it becomes almost visible.
    We're going to test that right now."

2. AS YOU LEAVE
   "Think of a specific object. Something that matters to you -- but not something
    anyone who knows you could easily guess. Picture it clearly, like a vivid photograph.
    Write the word."
   (hand them the billet and pen -- take both envelopes naturally as they take the pen)

3. FROM OUTSIDE (fill dead sound while they write)
   "When you're done, turn it face-down.
    Raise your hand -- when the audience sees that, they'll signal me to come back in."
   (switch small envelope outside -- return with windowed duplicate)

4. WHILE PEEKING (you slide small envelope into large)
   "We didn't set anything up beforehand, right?"
   (pocket the large envelope -- never opened again)

5. BOTH ON STAGE, FACING FORWARD
   "Imagine that object -- projected onto an enormous screen.
    Right in front of us. Fill the whole room with it."
   (pause -- study the imaginary screen)
   "This is the strong sense I'm getting..."
   (turn to whiteboard -- write associated word, cross it out, write actual word below)
   (hand board face-down to spectator)

6. REVEAL
   "I get a strong sense of [CATEGORY].
    My initial impression was [CROSSED-OUT WORD].
    What were you thinking of?"
   (they say the word)
   "I actually changed my mind.
    Have a look -- tell everyone if I was close."
   (they turn the board around)

WHITE DWARF  [opener]
=====================

[METHOD: You have two envelopes -- a small one and a large one. The small envelope
has an identical duplicate with a peek window on the back. Before the show, keep the
duplicate in your pocket.

Sequence:
  1. Spectator examines both envelopes (the real ones -- no window yet)
  2. You leave the room, taking the small envelope with you
  3. While outside, switch the small envelope for the windowed duplicate
  4. Return. Spectator writes their word on a card, seals it in the (switched) envelope
  5. They place the small envelope inside the large one
  6. During the handling/directions, get your peek through the window
  7. Reveal]

[TARGET TIME: 3 minutes. No more.]


There's a phenomenon called remote viewing. Military programs tested it for years --
the idea that a trained mind can perceive something happening in another location
without any physical connection to it. We're going to test it right now.

[hold up the two envelopes briefly]

Take a look at these. Small one, large one.

[hand to spectator for a quick check -- 5 seconds, not 30]

I'm going to leave the room. While I'm gone, I want you to think of anything --
a word, a name, a number, anything -- and write it on this card.

[hand them the card and pen]

Seal the card in the small envelope. Then put the small envelope inside the large one.
Hold onto it. I'll be back.

[exit -- switch the small envelope for the windowed duplicate -- return]

[take the large envelope, handle it to get the peek -- you now know the word]

[pause -- look at them, not at the envelope]

I want you to concentrate on what you wrote. Don't say it. Just hold it in your mind.

[beat]

[state the word or phrase confidently, as if you're reading it off the air in front of you]

It's [WORD].

Open it up.

[they open the envelope and pull out the card]

[silence -- let them show the room]

I wrote down what I sensed from the other side of that door.

[done]

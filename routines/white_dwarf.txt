WHITE DWARF: 3 minutes

PREMISE:
The human mind can project an image so vividly it becomes almost visible.

PROPS:
- Billet + pen
- 1 large envelope; 2 small (one windowed)
- Whiteboard + marker

PEOPLE:
- 1 spectator on stage for the full routine
- Call up: "[NAME] -- would you come up and help me?"
- Send back: "Thank you. As you head back to your seat."

1. INTRO
   Give spectator both envelopes to examine.
   As they hand them back, give them the billet and pen -- retain both envelopes.

   "A strong transmitting mind can project a thought so vividly it almost becomes visible.
    We're going to test that now."

2. AS I LEAVE THE ROOM
   "Think of any object.
    Something important to you specifically.
    Not something anyone could easily guess.
    Write it here."

3. FROM OUTSIDE (fill dead sound while they write)
   "When you're done, turn it face-down.
    Raise your hand -- when the audience sees that, they'll cheer to signal I should come back in."

4. WHILE PEEKING (as you slide small envelope into large -- pocket it immediately)
   "And we didn't set this up beforehand, right?"

5. BOTH ON STAGE, FACING FORWARD
   "Imagine a giant photograph of that object, projected onto a large screen,
    filling the whole room. This is the strong sense I'm getting."

   (Turn to whiteboard -- write associated word, cross it out, write actual word below)
   (Hand board face-down to spectator)

6. REVEAL
   "My initial impression was [CROSSED-OUT WORD].
    What were you thinking of?"
   (they answer)
   "I actually changed my mind. Tell everyone if I was close."
   (they turn board around)

TRANSITION:
"That's what happens when you think of an object. Now -- what if it's a human being."

WHITE DWARF  [opener]
=====================
TARGET TIME: 3 minutes


PREMISE
-------
The human mind can project a thought so vividly it becomes almost visible --
like an image on a screen. We're testing whether that image can be perceived
from another room, with no contact, no communication.


PROPS
-----
- Small envelope + large envelope (for spectator to examine)
- Windowed duplicate of small envelope (in pocket before show)
- Card + pen
- Whiteboard + dry erase marker
- Placard to display reveal


PEOPLE
------
- 1 spectator on stage (stays the whole routine)
- Send back: natural exit after reveal, no applause cue


LINK TO NEXT
------------
Both of you look forward together at the imaginary screen -- transition line:
"That image came from inside your mind. Now let's try something different --
what if it's not an image, but a name..."  [leads into Names and Place]


KEY PATTER -- hit these in order
---------------------------------
1. PREMISE LINE (before anything happens):
   "The mind can project a thought so vividly it becomes almost visible.
    We're going to test that right now."

2. CATEGORY (what to write -- make it meaningful, not random):
   "Think of something specific. Something that matters to you -- or something
    so clear you could photograph it. An object. A place. Something real.
    Write the word."

3. FROM OUTSIDE THE ROOM (while they write -- fills dead sound):
   "Take your time. Picture it clearly -- as if you're looking right at it.
    When you're done, turn it face-down and raise your hand."

4. CONFIRMATION (cover for the peek -- when you slide small into large):
   "We didn't set anything up beforehand, right?"

5. MOVIE SCREEN BUILD (both facing forward, hands raised):
   "Imagine what you wrote -- projected on an enormous screen.
    Right in front of us. Fill the whole wall with it."

6. TWO-STEP REVEAL (Magician in Trouble -- but only if it feels right for opener):
   "I'm getting something. It's not [ASSOCIATED WORD] --"
   [beat]
   "It's [ACTUAL WORD]."
   "Is that close, or exactly right?"
   "Turn it around. Show everybody."


DEAD MOMENT FILLS
-----------------
While spectator writes (you're outside): speak loud enough to be heard --
keep giving them instructions so there is no silence in the room.

While you write on the whiteboard: face away briefly -- say nothing,
let the silence build anticipation. This is the ONE place silence works for you.


METHOD REMINDER
---------------
Peek happens ONLY when you slide the small (windowed) envelope into the large one.
Window faces you. Pocket the large envelope immediately. It is never opened.
Whiteboard: write associated word, cross it out, write actual word below.
Hand face-down before the reveal.

WHITE DWARF  [opener]
=====================

[METHOD:
  - You have two envelopes: small and large, plus a windowed duplicate of the small one
  - Before the show: keep the windowed duplicate in your pocket

  Full sequence:
  1. Spectator examines both envelopes
  2. You take the small envelope and leave the room ("I'll take this with me")
  3. Spectator writes their word on a card while you're outside
  4. Outside: switch the real small envelope for the windowed duplicate
  5. Return. Hand them the (switched) small envelope -- they seal their card inside
  6. They place the sealed small envelope into the large envelope
  7. Handle the large envelope to get the peek through the window
  8. Reveal via placard using Magician in Trouble structure]

[TARGET TIME: 3 minutes. No more.]


There's a phenomenon called remote viewing. The idea that a trained mind can perceive
something in another location without any physical connection to it.
We're going to test it right now.

[hold up both envelopes briefly]

Take a look at these.

[hand to spectator -- 5 seconds max]

I'm going to leave the room. While I'm gone, think of anything at all -- a word,
a name, a number -- and write it on this card.

[hand them the card and pen]

I'll take the small envelope with me -- that way you can't say I coached you.
Hold onto the large one. I'll be back.

[exit with small envelope -- switch it for the windowed duplicate outside]

[return -- hand them the switched small envelope]

Seal your card inside this.

[they seal it]

Now put it inside the large one.

[they do -- you take the large envelope to handle it]

Just to confirm -- we didn't set anything up beforehand, right? You wrote whatever
came to mind, completely on your own?

[while they answer, get the peek through the window -- you now know the word]

[set the envelope aside -- pick up the marker and the placard, back turned slightly]

[write their FIRST LIKELY ASSOCIATION to the word -- cross it out -- write their actual word below]
[example: if the word is SHOE, write FOOT, cross it out, write SHOE beneath]

[hand the placard face-down to the spectator]

Hold that against your chest. Don't look at it yet.

[turn to face them]

My sense is you thought of something [GENERAL CATEGORY -- e.g. "related to the body,
the lower half"]. Not [CROSSED-OUT WORD] -- are you thinking of [ACTUAL WORD]?

[they confirm]

But be honest -- before you settled on that, did you first think of [CROSSED-OUT WORD]?

[they say yes]

Turn it around.

[they show the board -- crossed-out first word, correct word beneath]

[silence]

[OPTIONAL CLOSER -- use if it fits the moment:]
"I sensed both of your thoughts from the other side of that door."

[done]

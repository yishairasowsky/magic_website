WHITE DWARF  [opener]
=====================
TARGET TIME: 3 minutes


METHOD NOTES
------------
Props: small envelope, large envelope, windowed duplicate of small envelope (in pocket),
card, pen, marker, placard.

Peek happens when YOU place the small envelope into the large one -- window faces you
as you slide it in. That is the only moment you see the word. Pocket the large envelope
immediately after. It is never opened.

Reveal structure: write one close/associated word on the placard, cross it out,
write the actual word below it. Hand it face-down. Build to the turn.


-------- SCRIPT --------


The human mind is capable of projecting a thought so vividly that it becomes almost
visible. Like an image on a screen. We're going to test that right now.

    (Hold up both envelopes briefly)
    (Hand both to spectator -- 5 seconds to examine)
    (As they hand them back, give them the card and pen -- retain both envelopes naturally)

I'm going to step outside. While I'm gone, I want you to think of something --
something specific, something concrete. Something that could appear in a photograph.
Not an idea, not a feeling -- an object. Something a camera could capture.
Write the word on the card.

    (Exit with both envelopes -- outside, switch small for windowed duplicate)
    (From outside, loud enough to be heard:)

Take your time. Gaze at what you wrote for a few seconds -- picture it clearly.
When you're done, turn the card face-down and raise your hand.

    (Audience signals -- re-enter, stand next to spectator, both facing the audience)
    (Hold windowed envelope open yourself, flap toward them)

Slide it in.

    (They insert card -- you seal it)
    (YOU place small envelope into large -- GET PEEK as you slide it in)
    (Pocket the large envelope)

We didn't set anything up beforehand, right?

    (They confirm)
    (Pick up marker and placard -- write the associated word, cross it out, write actual word below -- facing yourself)
    (Hand placard face-down to spectator)

Please hold this. Don't look at it yet.

    (Both of you face forward -- raise your hands slightly, as if projecting the image out in front of you)

I want you to imagine what you wrote -- projected onto an enormous screen.
Right in front of us. Like a photograph blown up to fill the whole wall.

    (Pause -- study the imaginary screen)

I'm getting something. It's not [CROSSED-OUT WORD] --

    (Beat)

It's [ACTUAL WORD].

Is that close, or is that exactly right?

    (They confirm)

Turn it around. Show everybody.

    (They show the placard -- crossed-out word, correct word beneath)
    (Step back -- let the room react)

WHITE DWARF  [opener]
=====================
TARGET TIME: 3 minutes


METHOD NOTES
------------
Props: small envelope, large envelope, windowed duplicate of small envelope (in pocket),
card, pen, marker, placard.

Peek happens in step 5 -- when YOU place the small envelope into the large one.
The window faces you as you slide it in. That is the only moment you see the word.
The large envelope is then pocketed. It is never opened.


-------- SCRIPT --------


There's a phenomenon called remote viewing -- the idea that a trained mind can perceive
something at a distance, with no physical connection to it. We're going to test that now.

    (Hold up both envelopes briefly for the room to see)
    (Hand both to spectator -- 5 seconds to examine)
    (As they hand them back, hand them the card and pen -- retain both envelopes naturally,
     no comment about taking them)

I'm going to step outside. Write down anything.

    (Point to the card -- minimal emphasis)

Gaze at it for a few seconds.

    (Exit with both envelopes -- outside, switch small envelope for windowed duplicate)
    (From outside, loud enough to be heard:)

It could be anything -- a name, a place, a word, a number, a drawing.
Whatever comes to mind. When you're done, turn it face-down and raise your hand.

    (Audience signals you -- re-enter holding the windowed small envelope)
    (Hold it open yourself, flap facing them -- you control it, keep it flat)

Slide it in.

    (They insert the card -- you seal the envelope yourself)
    (YOU place the small envelope into the large one -- GET PEEK as you slide it in)
    (Pocket the large envelope)

Just to confirm -- you wrote that completely on your own?

    (They confirm)
    (Pick up marker and placard -- write the word, facing yourself)
    (Hand placard face-down to spectator)

Hold this. Don't look at it yet.

    (Step back -- face them)

You wrote [WORD].

    (Beat)

Turn it around.

    (They show the placard to the room -- silence -- done)

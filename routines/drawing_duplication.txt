DRAWING DUPLICATION
===================

I want everyone to think of something simple to draw. Just an image -- an object, an animal, a symbol, anything you like.

[NAME] -- I'm going to give you an envelope. Inside is a small card. I want you to draw your image on the card, seal it in the envelope, and hold onto it. Don't show anyone.

[spectator draws and seals]

I have not seen what you drew. No one in this room has seen it. It's sealed in that envelope in your hands.

[pause, concentrate]

You drew [DESCRIPTION].

Open the envelope. Show everyone.

[hold the silence -- don't rush past it]

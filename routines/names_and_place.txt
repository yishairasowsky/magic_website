NAMES AND PLACE (2BT -- TWO BILLET TEST)
========================================

I need two people for this. Two strangers -- people who don't know each other well.

[NAME 1] -- think of a person's name. Anyone at all. Someone you know, someone famous, anyone. Write it on this slip of paper, fold it, and hold it.

[NAME 2] -- think of a place. Anywhere in the world -- a city, a country, a specific location. Write it down, fold it, hold it.

[collect billets, work the one-ahead sequence]

[NAME 1] -- you wrote a name. The name you wrote is [NAME].

[NAME 2] -- you wrote a place. You wrote [PLACE].

[pause]

Two people. Two private thoughts. Written down, folded up, never shown to anyone. And yet here we are.

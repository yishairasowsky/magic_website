PK TOUCH
========

For this I need someone brave. Not physically brave -- emotionally brave. Someone willing to trust me completely, with their eyes closed, for about two minutes.

[NAME] -- come up please. Close your eyes. Whatever happens, keep them closed. And whatever you feel -- or think you feel -- tell the room out loud.

[touches: forehead x1, nose x3, plus secret touches]

Without opening your eyes -- am I touching you right now?

[Q1 -- pause for answer]

A moment ago -- was I touching you?

[Q2 -- pause for answer]

How many times did I touch that last spot?

[Q3 -- pause for answer]

Where was the last place I touched?

[Q4 -- pause for answer]

And this time -- same place as before, or somewhere different?

[Q5 -- go for "different," the harder call]

[NAME] -- open your eyes.

[to the room]

Her eyes were closed the entire time. She had no idea what was coming. And she got every single one right.

[lead the applause]

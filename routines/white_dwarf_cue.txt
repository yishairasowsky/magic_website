WHITE DWARF -- CUE SHEET

1.  "[NAME] -- would you come up and help me?"

2.  Hand both envelopes to spectator to examine.

3.  Hand billet + pen -- silently take both envelopes back.

4.  "A strong transmitting mind can project a thought so vividly it almost becomes visible.
     We're going to test that now."

5.  "Think of any object. Something important to you specifically.
     Not something anyone could easily guess. Write it here."

6.  EXIT. Switch small envelope for windowed duplicate.

7.  FROM OUTSIDE:
    "When you're done, turn it face-down.
     Raise your hand -- when the audience sees that, they'll cheer to signal I should come back in."

8.  ENTER. Hold windowed envelope open, flap toward them: "Slide it in."

9.  Seal the envelope yourself.

10. YOU slide small into large -- GET PEEK.

11. Pocket the large envelope.

12. "And we didn't set this up beforehand, right?"

13. Both face forward.
    "Imagine a giant photograph of that object, projected onto a large screen,
     filling the whole room. This is the strong sense I'm getting."

14. Turn to whiteboard. Write associated word. Cross it out. Write actual word below.

15. Hand board face-down to spectator: "Hold this."

16. "My initial impression was [CROSSED-OUT WORD].
     What were you thinking of?"

17. They answer.

18. "I actually changed my mind. Tell everyone if I was close."

19. They turn the board around.

20. "Thank you. As you head back to your seat."

21. TRANSITION:
    "That's what happens when you think of an object. Now -- what if it's a human being."

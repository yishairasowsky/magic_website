PHONE UNLOCK  [interrupt / standalone]
======================================

[NOTE: Ben's advice -- this works better as an interruption mid-routine than a standalone.
Consider dropping it in when someone in the audience challenges you or pulls out their phone.
The reveal must be visible to the whole room at once -- turn the phone toward the audience
the moment it opens. If they can't see the screen, the effect fails.]

[METHOD: You need to know the PIN in advance. Get it through whatever means fits your
performance context -- pre-show work, a force, a peek, etc.]


[A spectator comes up -- or challenges you -- to open their phone]

You're telling me I can't open this phone.

[take the phone -- or have them hold it facing you]

[enter the PIN]

[the moment it unlocks -- immediately turn the screen toward the audience so everyone
sees it open at the same time]

[say nothing -- let them process what they just saw]

[if they ask how -- shrug, or just move on]

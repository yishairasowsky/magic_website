## The Pitch

> "I offer an interactive virtual magic and mentalism show for kids — no setup, no travel, works on any device. I perform live over Zoom in English and tailor the show to your group. Perfect for Purim parties, Chanukah events, end-of-year celebrations, or any Sunday when the kids need something special."

**Key selling points:**
- Live and interactive — kids participate directly on screen
- No logistics: no venue, no travel, works on any device
- Based in Israel — adds a fun "magic from the Holy Land" angle
- Flexible scheduling: Israel evenings = US afternoons (East Coast friendly)
- Flat USD pricing, simple to book

**Pricing:** $350 per show (45–60 min). Can negotiate down to $275 if needed. Offer $250 for the very first US booking to get a testimonial.
**Lessons:** $90 per 40-min private Zoom lesson (one-on-one, any age/level).

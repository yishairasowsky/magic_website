I'm thinking we break it into 6 sections, flowing from Torah to modern day and ending with some fun reveals. Should run about 45-60 minutes total.


SECTION 1 — INTRO (5 min)

1. Yishai, tell us a bit about yourself. How does a frum Jew end up becoming a professional magician?


SECTION 2 — KISHUF IN THE TORAH (15 min)

2. The Torah explicitly forbids kishuf. So… are you violating the Torah right now?
3. What exactly does the Torah mean by kishuf? What are the relevant pesukim?
4. The Rambam and Ramban have a famous disagreement about whether kishuf is even real. Can you walk us through that?
5. If the Rambam holds it's all fakery, why does the Torah still forbid it?
6. So how do modern-day poskim view magic performances — is it actually mutar?


SECTION 3 — MAGIC IN CHAZAL (10 min)

7. Are there stories in Chazal that deal with magic or supernatural phenomena?
8. Let's talk about Pharaoh's magicians — as I discussed in my article, the Torah uses two different words for their methods. What was actually going on there?
9. The Gemara in Arvei Pesachim gets into sheidim, zugos, and some fascinating territory. What's going on in that sugya?


SECTION 4 — HOW MODERN MAGIC WORKS (10 min)

10. So how does modern magic actually work? What goes on behind the scenes that audiences don't know about?
11. Is there a psychological or human-nature element — why are we so easily fooled?


SECTION 5 — FAMOUS MAGICIANS (10 min)

12. Tell us about some famous magicians and what we can learn from their stories — I'm thinking Houdini, the Amazing Randi, and others.


SECTION 6 — SECRETS & STORIES (10 min)

13. Can you reveal how an actual magic trick works, right here on camera?
14. Do you have any memorable stories from your own performances?
15. Where can people find you and book you for their simcha?

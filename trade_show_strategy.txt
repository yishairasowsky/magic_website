TRADE SHOW AND EXHIBITION MARKET IN ISRAEL: STRATEGY

Short answer: yes, this is a real market and a good fit. Exhibitors pay a lot for a booth and the hardest problem is getting people to stop walking. A mentalist at the booth solves exactly that, and then the staff can talk to the crowd he gathers. It is a different buyer from the weddings and corporate event crowd, which means it adds a new pipeline instead of competing with the existing one.

WHO ACTUALLY HIRES FOR THIS (three different buyers)

1. The exhibitor (the company with the booth). This is the main buyer. The person who decides is usually the marketing manager or event manager at the company, sometimes the trade show agency they use. They book you for a day or for the whole show. Pitch: more people at your booth, more qualified leads, people stay longer and remember you.

2. The organizer (the company that runs the whole show). They sometimes hire entertainment for the opening, the networking evening, the VIP lounge, or walking entertainers across the floor. Fewer gigs but bigger and they can lead to repeat years.

3. Booth design and trade show agencies. They build booths for many exhibitors and can add a performer as an upsell. One agency relationship can give many bookings. Worth a short, direct message to each.

ORGANIZERS AND VENUES TO CONTACT (verify names, dates and contacts before sending, some of these may have changed)

Venues: Expo Tel Aviv (Israel Trade Fairs and Convention Center), Jerusalem International Convention Center (Binyanei HaUma), Eilat convention center, Haifa and Nazareth venues, Tel Aviv Convention Center at Tel Aviv University for conferences.

Organizers and show brands to look up: Ortra, Kenes Group, Isranet, Expo Tel Aviv's own events team, Fairs and Exhibitions Ltd, Hamashbir style retail events, Israel Export Institute events, Tel Aviv Cyber Week, AI Week, HLS and Cyber expo, Agritech, Intermedica and medical shows, MIXiii Biomed, food and kosher expos, wedding and simcha expos, home and design shows, hotel and hospitality expo, jewelry (IJEX), tourism (IMTM), Judaica and gift fairs, mobility and automotive shows, education and career fairs.

The best first step is to search for the annual exhibition calendar on Expo Tel Aviv's site and a few organizers' sites, pull the list of shows in the next 3 to 6 months, then pull the exhibitor list for each show. The exhibitor list is the real lead list.

WHAT TO OFFER (keep it concrete)

Booth draw: 20 to 40 minute sets of close-up mentalism at the booth, small groups gather around, staff hand out cards or scan badges at the end. Say exactly this in the pitch.

Branded effect: a prediction or reveal that ends with the company name, product, or a number tied to the brand. Ask for one line about the product and build one effect around it.

Lead capture: ask the audience for a name and phone number as part of the routine, or pass a badge scan after. Gives the exhibitor something measurable to report back to their boss.

Formats: full day at the booth, a few two-hour blocks, or roaming the floor. Offer a half-day option as the easy yes.

Opening or networking evening: for organizers, a short stage set plus roaming close-up.

PRICING IDEA (to decide with Yishai)

Per day rate for exhibitors, a lower half-day rate to make the first yes easy, and a higher organizer rate for evenings. Mention once that travel outside the center region adds a fee. Do not discount in the first message.

TIMING

Exhibitors book entertainment and plan booth activity weeks before a show, so contact exhibitors 3 to 6 weeks out when possible, and organizers several months out. Shows already in the next few weeks are still worth a last-minute short message to exhibitors who have not announced any activity.

WHAT I NEED FROM YISHAI TO START

1. A short video clip of a close-up set in a crowd or standing situation (not a seated dinner), if one exists. Booth work looks different from table work.
2. Whether he is willing to travel to Eilat, the north, and Jerusalem for this.
3. His day rate and half-day rate for exhibitions.
4. Whether he wants me to research the actual upcoming show calendar and exhibitor lists and build leads files like the earlier rounds (leads_new.txt style), then draft the pitches.

Step 1: Build the upcoming Israel exhibitions calendar for the next 6 months (name, dates, venue, organizer, contact).
Step 2: For the top 10 to 15 shows, pull exhibitor lists and marketing contacts.
Step 3: Write three pitch versions: exhibitor, organizer, booth design agency. All direct, soft tone, mentalist first, no exclamation marks.

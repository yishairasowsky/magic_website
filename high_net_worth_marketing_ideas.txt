Marketing Channels to Reach People With Money — Beyond What You're Already Doing

You're already doing: restaurant residency pitches, event planner outreach, corporate pitch decks, bar mitzvah outreach, Instagram content. Here's what's missing.

1. CONCIERGE NETWORKS

Hotels like David Citadel, King David, Mamilla Hotel, Orient Jerusalem — their concierge desks get asked "what should we do tonight?" every day. You don't need the hotel to hire you. You need the concierge to recommend you. Drop off a card, do a 5-minute demo for the concierge team in person, and offer them a referral fee per booking. Same goes for Airbnb luxury property managers — find the ones managing high-end apartments in Jerusalem and offer a "guest experience add-on" they can upsell.

2. PRIVATE TOUR GUIDES

High-end tour guides (especially those doing $500+/day private tours for Americans) are always looking for "wow" moments. Partner with 3-4 top guides — they mention you as an optional dinner add-on, you give them a cut. Reach out to guides on TripAdvisor's top-rated Jerusalem list or the Israel Guide College (Gvahim) network.

3. WEALTH-ADJACENT SERVICE PROVIDERS

These people already serve your ideal clients: private chefs, luxury car services, high-end florists, premium caterers (like Crave, Oded catering), interior designers for events. Offer cross-referral partnerships. They recommend you, you recommend them. Start with caterers — they're at every high-end event and always asked "do you know someone for entertainment?"

4. REAL ESTATE DEVELOPERS / BROKERS

Luxury real estate events (open houses, project launches, penthouse reveal parties) need sophisticated entertainment that matches the vibe. Reach out to developers marketing high-end Jerusalem projects (Tama 38 penthouses, Mamilla area, German Colony) and brokers like Anglo Saxon, RE/MAX luxury division. Position yourself as "experiential entertainment for client events" — not magic at a party.

5. CORPORATE HR / TEAM EVENTS DIRECT

Skip the event planners and go straight to HR departments and office managers at companies with Jerusalem offices. Tech companies (Check Point, Mobileye Jerusalem office, various startups), law firms, Big 4 accounting firms — they all do team events and client appreciation dinners. LinkedIn is the channel here. Post short clips of corporate performances and connect directly with HR managers.

6. PRIVATE CLUBS AND MEMBER GROUPS

Rotary, Lions Club, Freemasons, YPO (Young Presidents Organization), various business networking groups. These groups have monthly dinners and are always looking for speakers/entertainment. Offer to do one event free in exchange for exposure to 40 wealthy business owners in the room. The ROI on one free show at a YPO dinner could be enormous.

7. SYNAGOGUE DONOR DINNERS AND GALAS

You've done shul outreach for bar mitzvahs, but the real money is in the annual gala/dinner. Every major shul and Jewish institution has a fundraising dinner. The entertainment budget is already there. Pitch yourself to the dinner committee 2-3 months before the event. Focus on institutions with wealthy donor bases — Great Synagogue, Ramban, Yakar, Hebrew University donor events, Shaare Zedek/Hadassah hospital galas.

8. WEDDING VENDORS WHO SERVE THE HIGH END

Wedding photographers, videographers, luxury venue coordinators (Beit Shmuel, Sergei Courtyard, Olive & Vine) — they talk to engaged couples in the planning phase. "We know a mentalist who does incredible cocktail hour entertainment" is a natural upsell. Join a wedding vendor WhatsApp group or attend a bridal expo as a vendor.

9. SHORT-FORM VIDEO THAT TARGETS THE RIGHT AUDIENCE

Not just posting on Instagram — create content specifically designed to reach event planners, brides, and corporate clients. Film performance clips at recognizable luxury venues (even if unpaid). Use location tags for David Citadel, Mamilla, etc. The algorithm will push it to people who frequent those places. Also: post on LinkedIn (corporate buyers scroll LinkedIn, not Instagram).

10. GOOGLE ADS — "MAGICIAN JERUSALEM" / "ENTERTAINER FOR CORPORATE EVENT ISRAEL"

People with money who need entertainment Google it. A small Google Ads budget ($10-15/day) targeting searches like "magician for event Jerusalem," "corporate entertainment Israel," "bar mitzvah entertainer" can put you in front of people actively looking to book. Your website needs a clear booking form for this to convert.

WHAT TO DO FIRST

Pick the highest-leverage, lowest-effort move: concierge outreach. Walk into 3 luxury hotels this week with your card and a 30-second pitch to the front desk. "I'm a mentalist. Your guests ask you for dinner recommendations — I perform at the table. Can I show you something?" Then show them one trick. That's it.

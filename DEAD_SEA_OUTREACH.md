## How to Approach

1. **Don't cold-call the front desk.** Ask specifically for the *events coordinator* or *entertainment manager*.
2. **Lead with the video.** Have your highlight reel ready to send by WhatsApp or email immediately after the call.
3. **Offer one free Shabbat afternoon slot** to let them see how guests react — this gets you in the door.
4. **Follow up once** after 1 week if no response. After that, move on to the next hotel.

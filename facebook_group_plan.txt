GROUP NAME: Anglo Event Planning Israel

DESCRIPTION:
A community for English-speaking olim and expats in Israel to share tips, recommendations, and questions about planning events — Bar/Bat Mitzvahs, weddings, corporate events, fundraisers, Chanukah parties, community nights, and everything in between. Whether you're looking for a venue, caterer, photographer, DJ, entertainment, or just advice from people who've been through it — this is the place. All recommendations and questions welcome!

FIRST POST:
Welcome to Anglo Event Planning Israel! 🎉

This group is for anyone in the Anglo community planning an event in Israel and looking for advice, recommendations, or inspiration.

A few ideas for what to post:
- Looking for a venue in Jerusalem/Tel Aviv/wherever? Ask here.
- Found an amazing caterer or photographer? Share the love.
- Planning a Bar Mitzvah and don't know where to start? We've got you.
- Need entertainment ideas for a fundraiser or corporate event? Let's brainstorm.

Ground rules:
1. Be helpful and respectful
2. Recommendations are welcome — spam is not
3. If you're a vendor, feel free to introduce yourself once and then be a helpful member of the community

Looking forward to building this together!

UPCOMING ISRAEL EXHIBITIONS (researched 2026-11-04)

Important: all dates below come from aggregator sites, not the organizers, and several conflict or are marked TBD. Confirm each one on the organizer's site before pitching. The official venue calendar to check first is expotelaviv.co.il. Notes on fit are my own judgment.

NEXT FEW WEEKS (exhibitors still planning booth activity)

Israfood, Bake and Cake, HOTEX and KITEX: November 17 to 18, 2026, Expo Tel Aviv. Food, bakery, hotel and restaurant equipment, institutional kitchens. Four co-located shows means hundreds of exhibitors in one building on the same days. Best target right now, very short notice so pitch exhibitors this week. HOTEX is also hospitality, which matches the luxury hotel and restaurant angle. KITEX is professional visitors only.

Fresh Agro-Mashov: November 9 to 10, 2026, Aliba in Ofakim. Agriculture, too close and probably too niche. Low priority.

ISPRINT (printing technology): November 3 to 4, 2026. Already happening or over, skip.

Vision and AI for Industrial Automation plus a motion control and robotics event: December 15, 2026, Expo Tel Aviv. Only one source, verify. Technical audience, smaller crowd, medium priority.

EARLY 2027

Cybertech Global 2027: January 25 to 27, 2027, Expo Tel Aviv. Cyber conference and exhibition, big corporate crowd and many booths with lead generation budgets. One source had conflicting info, verify. High priority.

GYM Israel (sport, fitness, health): listed for February 2027, dates TBD. Medium priority.

IMTM 2027 (Mediterranean tourism market): February 16 to 17, 2027, Expo Tel Aviv. Tourism and hospitality buyers, good fit for the luxury hospitality angle. High priority.

Aclima, RAX and NEWACLIMA (HVAC, refrigeration, electrical, energy): March 2 to 3, 2027, Expo Tel Aviv. Organized by Stier Group. Co-located shows listed with it include ANALIZA, ABILITECH, POOLS and others. Exhibitors are industrial and less flashy, but the crowd is large. Medium priority. Note another source said RAX in May 2027, so verify.

MIXiii Health-Tech: was March 2026, check for the 2027 edition.

SPRING AND SUMMER 2027

Biomed Israel 2027: May 18 to 20, 2027, Tel Aviv, venue not confirmed. Medical and biotech. Medium priority.

Construct and Design Exhibition 2027: June 1 to 2, 2027, Tel Aviv, venue not confirmed. Construction, design, architecture. Medium priority, design exhibitors tend to care about booth experience.

Timber Israel 2027, Israfood 2027, HOTEX-KITEX 2027: listed at Expo Tel Aviv with dates TBD. Check the 2027 dates when announced.

NOT YET FOUND, STILL TO CHECK

Wedding and simcha expos, home and design shows, jewelry (IJEX), Judaica and gift fairs, kosher food expos, education and career fairs, Jerusalem ICC events, Eilat events, Cyber Week at Tel Aviv University, AI Week, mobility and EcoMotion (postponed, dates unset), defense and homeland security shows. Wedding and simcha expos in particular could be a direct fit since exhibitors there are venues and vendors who book entertainment.

RECOMMENDED ORDER

1. Israfood, HOTEX, KITEX, Bake and Cake (Nov 17 to 18): pitch exhibitors immediately. Realistic only for a few, but it also tests whether the pitch works.
2. IMTM and Cybertech (Jan and Feb): pitch exhibitors starting mid December. Also pitch the organizers about opening events.
3. Aclima, Biomed, Construct and Design, GYM (Feb to June): pitch about 4 to 6 weeks before each.
4. Booth design and trade show agencies: pitch any time, one relationship covers many shows.

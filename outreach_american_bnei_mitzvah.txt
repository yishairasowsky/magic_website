American Bar/Bat Mitzvah in Israel — Outreach Plan

WHO TO TARGET

1. US-based bar mitzvah trip organizers / Israel tour operators
- Ayelet Tours (ayelet.com) — huge in bar mitzvah family trips
- Israel Discovery Tours
- Da'at Educational Expeditions
- Keshet: The Center for Educational Tourism in Israel
- Makor Educational Journeys

2. American synagogues with Israel bar mitzvah programs
Many Conservative and Reform congregations run group bar/bat mitzvah trips to Israel. The rabbi or youth director is the contact. Focus on:
- Large suburban congregations in NY/NJ, LA, South Florida, Chicago, DC area
- Synagogues that advertise "Israel bar mitzvah trip" on their website

3. US-based event planners who specialize in Israel destination events
- Search "bar mitzvah in Israel planner" — several American planners coordinate the whole thing from the US side

4. Luxury Israel hotels that host bar mitzvah celebrations
- King David Jerusalem, Waldorf Astoria Jerusalem, Mamilla Hotel, David Citadel
- These hotels have events departments that get asked "do you know a performer?"

5. American Jewish Facebook groups / online communities
- "Bar Mitzvah in Israel" groups
- "Jewish Families Traveling to Israel" groups
- Nefesh B'Nefesh community boards (lots of American families with Israel connections)

WHAT TO SAY

Core pitch angle: "Your family is flying to Israel for the biggest milestone of your son's life. The Kotel ceremony is handled. The dinner venue is booked. But what makes the celebration itself unforgettable? A mentalist who reads minds — in English — and leaves every guest stunned."

Key selling points for American families:
- Performs in English (critical — they're worried about a language barrier with Israeli vendors)
- Mentalism, not magic — sophisticated, not a kids' clown
- Perfect for the private dinner / party portion of the trip
- Has performed for international luxury clients in Jerusalem
- Can tailor the show to the bar mitzvah boy/girl (learn details in advance, incorporate them)
- Professional, reliable, Western-standard communication (Americans booking in Israel are nervous about vendor responsiveness)
